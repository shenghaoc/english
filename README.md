# English

This repo is a collection of properties of standard English that prevent me from accepting well-meaning English language advice.

English dictionaries are descriptive rather than prescriptive, however the varieties that are most well-described are also far easier to learn than those that are not. 

When there is no clear boundary between the local creole and the local variety of English it is impossible for a learner to tell whether his imitation sounds too crass or pompous. 

The creole is heard in everday life but the local variety of English tends to only be used for more formal settings and its exact quality seems to depend on how much the speaker is willing to put on a 'fake accent'. In fact, I have come to realize that I cannot differentiate those with a 'fake accent' and those who speak a certain way because of international exposure. Both seem to imitate a vaguely familiar rhythm without knowing which syllable should be stressed.

Inherently, when learning a language, there are certain unfamiliar features that you can never fully understand and you would have to make do with approximations. Now, what if you are asked to approximate someone else's approximations, almost like a game of Chinese whispers?
